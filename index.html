FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ProofXShield and Midnight zero-knowledge sealed-bid auctions.

How are my bids kept confidential?

Your bid amount and secret salt are processed locally inside your browser using WebAssembly proving keys. Only a cryptographic commitment hash is submitted to Midnight's private ledger.

What wallet do I need to use ProofXShield?

You need a Midnight-compatible browser extension wallet such as Lace (Midnight edition) or 1AM Wallet connected to the Midnight Preprod testnet.

Can an auction creator or miner tamper with results?

No. Zero-knowledge proofs mathematically enforce the auction rules on-chain. Winning outcomes are verified against submitted commitments without exposing unrevealed data.

How do I create or join an auction?

You can create a new auction round via the Create Auction page, or place sealed bids on existing rounds via the Place Bid page.
